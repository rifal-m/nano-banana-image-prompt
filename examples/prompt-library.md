# Prompt Library

Prompts that have proven themselves, ready to copy. Add a new one every time you find
something that works well — this repo's value grows out of this collection.

The shorthand **[RIDER]** below means the following block, pasted whole at the end of
the prompt:

```
Maintain perfect identity. Show realistic pores, skin micro-texture, authentic skin
translucency, realistic subsurface scattering, professional full-frame camera quality,
sharp focus on the eyes, cinematic realism, detailed facial features, realistic skin
rendering, 8K HDR, editorial photography quality.
```

## Editorial portrait, window light

Model: Nano Banana Pro · Ratio: 4:5 · For: a profile page

> An editorial half-body portrait of Aleira — 28, oval face, warm olive undertone, dark
> almond eyes, a small mole above her left lip — standing beside a tall window in a Bali
> co-working space, mid-sentence and half-turned toward someone off frame. Late afternoon
> light rakes in from camera left, leaving a rectangular window catchlight in her eyes and
> a soft falloff across her right cheek. Shot on a full-frame camera, 85mm at f/1.8, eye
> level. Fine flyaway hairs catch the light along her part, faint redness across the bridge
> of her nose, a linen shirt creased at the elbow. For a company profile page. [RIDER]

Why it works: the action (mid-sentence) prevents an empty pose, the catchlight has a
named shape, and three imperfections are called out.

## Candid office scene, multiple characters

Model: Nano Banana 2 · Ratio: 16:9 · For: a website hero

> A documentary-style wide shot of three colleagues around a cluttered desk in a Jakarta
> startup office at dusk — Gita seated and laughing with her head tipped back, Baskara
> leaning over the monitor pointing at something, Aleira standing behind them with a mug,
> listening. Warm practical light from a desk lamp just out of frame, mixed with cool blue
> ambient from the window behind. Full-frame camera, 35mm at f/2.8, from across the room.
> Cable slack under the desk, a mug ring on a printout, chairs at uneven angles. [RIDER]

Why it works: every character has a different action, the light mixes warm and cool
temperatures, and the environment is deliberately untidy.

## Dramatic close-up, overcast light

Model: Nano Banana Pro · Ratio: 1:1

> A tight editorial close-up of Aleira on a covered walkway during heavy overcast, rain
> blurred in the background. Soft directionless daylight with no visible catchlight ring,
> only a broad sky reflection across the upper iris. Full-frame camera, 85mm at f/2.0,
> slightly above eye level. Damp hair at the temples, a faint sheen on the forehead,
> uneven skin tone across the cheeks, one strand of hair crossing her eyebrow. [RIDER]

Why it works: overcast light easily looks fake — the explicitly named sky reflection is
what saves it.

## Half-body at work, practical light at night

Model: Nano Banana 2 · Ratio: 9:16 · For: a social media story

> A candid vertical frame of Baskara at a desk late at night, face lit from below by the
> monitor with a warm desk lamp filling from camera right, the rest of the room falling
> into darkness. He is reading, not posing, one hand resting near the keyboard. Full-frame
> camera, 50mm at f/1.8. Stubble catching the rim light along the jaw, tired skin under the
> eyes, a collar sitting slightly off centre. [RIDER]

Why it works: being lit from below by a monitor is a real situation that rarely shows up
in generic AI images.
